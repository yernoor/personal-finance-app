# Widgets

Six home-screen widgets show your spending at a glance. **They show spending, never account balances.**

## Add one
1. Long-press an empty spot on the home screen and choose **Widgets**.
2. Find **Money Tracker** and pick a widget.
3. Drag it where you want it. Tap it to open the matching screen.

| Widget | Default size | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Spent this month | 2×2 | Spending this month against the forecast, with a limit-warning dot |
| Spent today | 2×2 | Today's spending and the last operations |
| Quick add | 2×1 | One button that opens the add screen for the kind you chose when placing it |
| Currency rates | 2×2 | Up to three currencies against tenge, with a refresh button |
| Month by category | 4×4 | The month's stacked category bar and top categories |
| Month dashboard | 4×6 | Totals, chart, limits and recent operations in one card |

## Settings (More → Widgets)
- **Widget style**: *Nothing OS* (light translucent fill, dot-matrix numbers), *Glass* (frosted fill) or *Usual* (opaque card). All follow light and dark mode.

Widgets refresh after every change you save and every 30 minutes.

## Known limits
- The picker previews use made-up numbers; on Android 14 and older the app icon shows instead.
- Only the *Month at a glance* widget has been looked at on an emulator; the others and the phone itself are not yet checked. On some launchers the tall dashboard is offered smaller than 4×6.
